This usability and UX enhancement study demonstrates the critical role of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) principles in digital interface design. By systematically identifying structural flaws and applying visual, perceptual, and cognitive design methodologies, the proposed improvements deliver the following key outcomes:

* **Reduced Cognitive Load:** Streamlined layouts and step-by-step wizard flows minimize operational friction and decision fatigue for users across both mobile and self-service platforms.
* **Enhanced Task Completion Rates:** High-visibility call-to-action (CTA) buttons, persistent cart shortcuts, and clear summary dialogs reduce drop-off rates and improve transactional speed.
* **Improved Accessibility & Readability:** Standardized typographic hierarchy and high-contrast visual elements comply with WCAG guidelines, ensuring seamless usability in varied lighting and high-noise public environments.
* **Error Minimization:** Clear progress indicators and pre-transaction verification screens prevent accidental user selections and reduce processing errors.

Overall, integrating human-centered design frameworks significantly enhances overall user satisfaction, accessibility, and systemic efficiency across e-commerce and public service infrastructure.
