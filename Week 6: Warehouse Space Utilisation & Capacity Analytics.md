This repository contains the complete operational, capacity, and space utilisation analytics across the distribution network[cite: 1]. ## Key Operational Objectives
* **Throughput & Capacity Analytics:** Conduct comparative volume evaluations to balance inbound Purchase Orders (POs) with outbound Sales Orders (SOs)[cite: 1].
* **Operational Bottleneck Identification:** Pinpoint core drivers causing execution delays, such as receiving dock congestion, floor-load stress, and aisle blockage[cite: 1].
* **Warehouse-to-Product Alignment:** Align high-velocity SKUs vs. heavy/bulky inventory with appropriate spatial and racking configurations[cite: 1].
* **Capacity Heatmap Development:** Map capacity stress levels across core SKU categories (Undercarriage, Hydraulics, Transmission, Seals & Lubricants)[cite: 1].

## Key Findings & Analytical Insights

### 1. Capacity Stress & Spatial Utilization
* High-demand mega distribution hubs operate near maximum operational thresholds, limiting safety staging buffers[cite: 1].
* Heavy regional hubs face floor-load limits and crane availability bottlenecks while handling dense machinery parts[cite: 1].
* Regional depots suffer from floor space lockup due to slow-moving and aged inventory, reducing prime rack availability[cite: 1].

### 2. Throughput & Flow Dynamics
* High picking-velocity hubs demonstrate fast turnover for precision components like Seal Kits and Control Valves[cite: 1].
* Coastal import hubs face intermittent buffer congestion linked to shipment container arrival spikes[cite: 1].
* Vertical airspace remains underutilized across multiple industrial hubs due to reliance on single-tier floor stacking[cite: 1].

## Actionable Strategic Recommendations
1. **Implement ABC-Volumetric Slotting:** Re-zone dynamic picking zones so high-velocity items are located near dispatch bays[cite: 1].
2. **Heavy-Equipment Cantilever Racking:** Install cantilever racking to clear ground-level staging areas from loose bulk stacking[cite: 1].
3. **Inventory Rebalancing & Liquidation:** Transfer aged inventory from low-turnover depots to high-demand hubs to recover floor buffer capacity[cite: 1].
4. **Dock Flow Optimization:** Restructure inbound staging buffer zones to reduce vehicle turnaround delays during peak arrival hours[cite: 1].
