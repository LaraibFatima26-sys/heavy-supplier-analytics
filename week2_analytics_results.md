# Week 2 Results — Heavy Supplier, Inventory & Warehouse Analytics

## Project Results

The Week 2 analysis focused on cleaning operational data, automating calculations, and preparing procurement, sales, and product inventory data for business intelligence reporting.

### 1. Purchase Orders & Supplier Performance
- Automated purchase order fulfillment status using Excel formulas.
- Calculated supplier lead times using purchase and receipt dates.
- Corrected date formatting and resolved spreadsheet calculation errors.

### 2. Sales & Profitability Analysis
- Calculated Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).
- Derived profit amounts and gross profit percentages.
- Standardized transaction dates for time-based sales analysis.

### 3. Product & Inventory Analytics
- Summarized total units sold and revenue by product ID using `SUMIF`.
- Implemented ABC classification based on cumulative revenue contribution.
- Organized products into Fast-Moving, Medium-Moving, Slow-Moving, and Dead Stock categories.

### 4. Data Quality Improvements
- Resolved `#VALUE!` errors.
- Standardized date formats.
- Removed redundant date columns.
- Implemented dynamic formulas to improve calculation consistency.

A consolidated Excel analytical workbook, `heavy_supplier_inventory_analytics_week2.xlsx`, was prepared to support supplier performance monitoring, sales profitability analysis, and product-level inventory insights.
